Privacy Policy

This policy covers Neuronauts AI Publisher, the internal tool Neuronauts AI uses to publish its own educational posts to its own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X.

Who uses it

The tool is used only by the Neuronauts AI team to post to accounts we own. It is not offered to the public, and no other people sign in to it.

What data it handles

  • Sign-in tokens for our own accounts, issued by each platform when we authorize the tool. They are stored only on our own computer and are used only to upload our posts, captions, thumbnails and subtitles, to post comments or replies on our own posts, and to read basic information about our own accounts (such as the account name and posting limits).
  • Our own content: the videos, images and captions we create.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store, analyze or sell personal data about viewers, followers or any other users.
  • It does not read other people's messages, profiles or content.
  • It does not share any platform data with third parties.

Deleting data

We can revoke the tool's access at any time from each platform's account settings, and we delete the stored tokens when the tool is no longer used. If you believe the tool holds any data about you, write to us and we will delete it.
